Entry for make.fun `news-summary-01` ($200, 20 places). `main.ts` (Node 22,
stdlib only, no network) copies `summary.txt` to `/output/entry.txt`.

The summary covers eight verified stories from 11 November 2026, ordered
by importance: the Abu Dhabi Russia-Ukraine talks, the COP31 leaders'
summit, US charges over Iran-backed plots, the Hegseth impeachment vote
and the Mississippi Senate call, Saudi Arabia's interception of Houthi

BBC, UN, US Justice Department, New York Times); nothing is invented.
